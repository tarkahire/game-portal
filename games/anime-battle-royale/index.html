HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Be the last fighter alive among 9.

Storm: A neon ring shrinks every 30 seconds. Stay inside or take damage.

Controls

  • WASD — move
  • Mouse — aim camera (click to lock)
  • Left click — M1 melee attack
  • Z / X / C / V — abilities 1-4
  • F — mobility ability
  • Shift — sprint
  • Space — dash (no cooldown, brief i-frames)
  • ESC — pause
